Ride-Book Rider — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated 18 August 2026 · Contact: support@ridebook.example

Information We Collect

Account details (name, phone), ride request details (pickup and drop-off addresses, route, timing), and device/crash diagnostics needed to operate and improve the service.

How We Use Your Information

We use it exclusively to: match you with drivers, quote fares, provide navigation and tracking, complete payments, ensure safety (OTP verification, SOS), and provide support. We do NOT use your data for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Location & Permissions

We ask for your location ONLY when you explicitly choose to use the current location for a ride. Your location is never collected in the background. You can revoke location permission any time in your device settings.

Sharing

We share only what is required to complete your ride (pickup/drop-off and live ride status) with the matched driver, and with service providers who help us operate (payments, maps). No third-party marketing sharing.

Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored securely. Access is limited to staff who need it to operate the service.

Retention & Deletion

We keep ride records only as long as required. You may request a copy of your data or its deletion at any time at support@ridebook.example.

Your Rights & Contact

You can access, correct, or delete your personal data, and object to processing. To exercise these rights, email support@ridebook.example.
