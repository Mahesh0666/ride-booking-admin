Ride-Book Partner — Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.0 · Updated 18 August 2026 · Contact: maheshbabuv57@gmail.com

Information We Collect

Account details (name, phone), verification documents (driving licence, vehicle registration, insurance, photo), vehicle information, live location while you are online, ride history, and support communications.

How We Use Your Information

We use it exclusively to: verify your identity for rider safety, match you with ride requests near you, let riders track your arrival, calculate fares and earnings, and provide support. We do NOT use your data for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Location & Permissions

While you are online, Ride-Book Partner collects your GPS location continuously — including in the background — so you receive nearby ride requests and so riders can track your approach. This is the core of the service you are providing. You can go offline or stop sharing location any time.

Sharing

We share your name, photo, vehicle details, and live ride position with the rider who books with you, and with service providers who help us operate (payments, maps, analytics). No third-party marketing sharing.

Security

Your data is transmitted over encrypted connections and stored securely. Access is limited to staff who need it to operate the service.

Retention & Deletion

We retain verification documents and ride records while your account is active and as required by law. You may request a copy or deletion of your data any time at maheshbabuv57@gmail.com.

Your Rights & Contact

You can access, correct, or delete your personal data, and object to processing. To exercise these rights, email maheshbabuv57@gmail.com.
