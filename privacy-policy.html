Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 15, 2026 | Last Updated: September 15, 2026

Ride-Book ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Ride-Book mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Registration: Name, phone number, email address, password
  • Profile Information: Profile photo, saved addresses
  • Payment Information: Payment method preference (processed by Razorpay; we do not store card details)
  • Driver Information (Partner App only): Driving license number, vehicle details (make, model, year, color, license plate), vehicle registration documents, insurance documents
  • Communications: Support requests, ratings, and reviews

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: GPS coordinates for ride pickup, drop-off, and real-time tracking during active rides. Drivers share location while online.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system, app version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: App features used, session duration, crash logs

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication Providers: If you use phone number verification, we receive authentication status from Twilio
  • Payment Processors: Payment confirmation from Razorpay

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our ride-booking services
  • Match riders with nearby drivers and process ride requests
  • Provide real-time ride tracking and navigation
  • Process payments and send payment receipts
  • Send ride updates, OTP verification codes, and service notifications via SMS
  • Verify driver identities and vehicle documents
  • Provide customer support
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraud
  • Comply with legal obligations

3.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or governmental request.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained while account is active
  • Ride history: Retained for 2 years for support and legal purposes
  • Location data: Retained for 90 days after ride completion
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years as required by Indian tax law
  • Driver documents: Retained while driver account is active

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption of data in transit (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Encrypted password storage (bcrypt)
  • JWT-based authentication with secure tokens
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Regular security audits

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Opt-out: Opt-out of non-essential data collection

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the App: Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  • By Email: Send a request to maheshbabuv57@gmail.com

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be deleted within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy

Ride-Book is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

9. Location Permissions

Rider App: We access your location when you request a ride to set pickup and drop-off points. Location is shared with your assigned driver during the ride.

Partner App: We access your location while you are online to match you with nearby ride requests. Location is shared with riders during active rides. Background location is used to maintain ride service when the app is minimized.
